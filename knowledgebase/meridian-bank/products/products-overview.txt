MERIDIAN BANK — PRODUCT OVERVIEW
Knowledge Base Document | Version 1.0

=============================================================
FULL PRODUCT CATALOGUE
=============================================================

CURRENT ACCOUNTS:
- Everyday Current Account: Free, Visa debit, online and mobile banking, overdraft available.
- Premium Current Account: £12/month, all Everyday features plus travel insurance, mobile phone insurance, no FX fees, 0.5% AER on balances up to £2,500, priority service line, free CHAPS.
- Basic Bank Account: Free, no credit check, Visa debit, no overdraft. For customers who do not qualify for standard accounts.

SAVINGS ACCOUNTS:
- Instant Access Savings Account: 3.10% AER variable, no minimum (£1), unlimited access, linked to current account.
- 1-Year Fixed Rate Bond: 4.65% AER fixed, £1,000 minimum, no access during term, higher rate for locking in funds for a year.
- 2-Year Fixed Rate Bond: 4.90% AER fixed, £1,000 minimum, best rate in the range, no access for 2 years.
- Cash ISA: 3.75% AER variable, tax-free interest, up to £20,000/year, instant access, flexible ISA (withdraw and re-deposit in same year without losing allowance).
- Junior ISA: 4.00% AER variable, for children under 18, managed by parent/guardian, locked until age 18.

CREDIT CARDS:
- Everyday Rewards Mastercard: £0 annual fee, 22.9% APR, earn Meridian Points (1pt/£1; 1,000pts=£10 cashback), up to 56 days interest-free on purchases.
- Premium Travel Mastercard: £15/month, 19.9% APR, no FX fees, travel insurance, airport lounge access (4 visits/year), concierge service.

DEBIT CARDS:
- Visa Debit (Everyday Account): standard UK and international use, contactless £100, daily ATM limit £500, FX fee applies.
- Visa Debit (Premium Account): all above, plus no FX fees and free overseas ATM withdrawals to £500/month.

MORTGAGES (illustrative rates — subject to change):
- 2-Year Fixed Rate: 4.25% (then SVR 7.49%)
- 5-Year Fixed Rate: 4.10% (then SVR 7.49%)
- 10-Year Fixed Rate: 4.35% (then SVR 7.49%)
- 2-Year Tracker: Bank of England Base Rate + 0.89%
- Maximum LTV: 90%. Capital-and-interest repayment or interest-only (max 75% LTV, subject to repayment vehicle).
- New mortgage enquiries: referred to qualified mortgage adviser.

DIGITAL BANKING:
- Meridian Bank Mobile App (iOS and Android): account management, payments, card controls, mortgage management, secure messaging.
- Online Banking: meridianbank.co.uk — full account management, export to OFX/CSV, mortgage calculator.

=============================================================
WHICH PRODUCT IS RIGHT FOR THE CUSTOMER?
=============================================================

Customers who want free everyday banking:       → Everyday Current Account
Customers who travel or want added benefits:    → Premium Current Account
Customers with a poor credit history:           → Basic Bank Account
Customers wanting easy-access savings:          → Instant Access Savings Account
Customers wanting highest possible savings rate:→ 2-Year Fixed Rate Bond (if they can lock away funds)
Customers wanting tax-free savings:             → Cash ISA
Customers saving for a child's future:          → Junior ISA
Customers who spend a lot on credit:            → Everyday Rewards Mastercard (rewards points)
Customers who travel frequently:                → Premium Travel Mastercard (no FX fees, travel insurance)
Customers buying or remortgaging a property:    → Mortgage — refer to adviser

ARIA does not make product recommendations for mortgages. All mortgage advice is provided by a qualified Meridian Bank mortgage adviser.
ARIA does not provide regulated financial advice (investments, pensions, insurance other than what is included with products).

=============================================================
PRODUCT ELIGIBILITY SUMMARY
=============================================================

Everyday Current Account:   UK resident, 18+, photo ID, proof of address, soft credit check
Premium Current Account:    UK resident, 18+, as above; or upgrade from Everyday Account
Basic Bank Account:         UK resident, 18+, photo ID, proof of address, no credit check
Savings (all):              UK resident, 18+ (or parent/guardian for Junior ISA), current account required for Instant Access
Credit Cards:               UK resident, 18+, credit assessment (hard credit check), minimum income £10,000/year
Mortgages:                  UK resident, 18+, income and affordability assessment, property in England or Wales
