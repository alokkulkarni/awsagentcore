=============================================================
BOOKING AN APPOINTMENT
=============================================================

For mortgage advice, financial review, or complex account queries, it is recommended to book an appointment in advance.

How to book:
- Mobile app: More → Find a branch → select branch → "Book an appointment" → choose service type, date, and time.
- Online banking: Help → Find a branch → Book appointment.
- Call the main number 0161 900 9000 and ask to book an appointment at your preferred branch.
- Walk-in appointments are accommodated when advisers are available, but waiting times can be significant during busy periods (Monday mornings and last week of the month).

What to bring to a branch appointment:
- Photo ID (passport or full UK driving licence)
- Proof of address dated within 3 months (utility bill, council tax bill, or bank statement from another provider)
- For mortgage appointments: payslips (last 3 months), bank statements (last 3 months), details of any existing mortgages/loans

ACCESSIBILITY
=============================================================

All Meridian Bank branches are wheelchair accessible with level or ramped access, wide doorways, and hearing loops at all service counters.

- Step-free access: all branches
- Hearing loops: all service counters and mortgage/adviser rooms
- Large-print literature: available at all branches on request
- Signing available: BSL interpreter can be pre-booked via 0161 900 9006 (textphone) or via secure message with at least 48 hours' notice
- Home visit: for customers who are unable to visit a branch due to illness or disability, a home visit can be arranged. Call 0161 900 9000 to discuss.

=============================================================
FOREIGN CURRENCY
=============================================================

Available currencies (standard stock): GBP, EUR, USD, CHF, JPY, AUD, CAD, SEK, NOK, DKK, NZD, SGD, AED

Pre-ordering:
- For amounts over £500 equivalent or for less common currencies: pre-order at least 2 working days in advance.
- Pre-order via the mobile app (More → Order foreign currency), online banking, or by calling 0161 900 9000.

Exchange rates:
- In-branch rates are set daily based on wholesale market rates plus a 2.75% margin (Everyday Account) or 0.75% (Premium Account).
- Rates displayed on the branch board or at meridianbank.co.uk/exchange-rates.
