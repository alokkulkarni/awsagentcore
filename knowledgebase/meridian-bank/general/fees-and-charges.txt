HOW FEES ARE CHARGED
=============================================================

- Monthly account fees are collected by direct debit on the last working day of each month.
- Transaction fees (e.g. CHAPS, card replacement) are debited from your current account on the day of the transaction.
- Credit card fees (late payment, balance transfer) appear on your next monthly statement.
- Mortgage fees (ERC, deeds release) are deducted from the mortgage account or redemption proceeds.

If a fee charge causes your account to go overdrawn, the standard overdraft terms apply.

=============================================================
FEE WAIVERS AND EXCEPTIONS
=============================================================

Meridian Bank may waive fees in the following circumstances:
- First-time late payment fee (credit card): may be waived on request if account is otherwise in good standing. Call 0161 900 9000.
- Returned payment fee: first occurrence may be waived if payment is subsequently made within 24 hours.
- Customers experiencing financial difficulty: contact 0161 900 9000. Our team has discretion to waive or defer charges.
- Bereavement: all fees are waived for a period of 3 months following the notification of a deceased account holder.
- Vulnerable customers: fee decisions are made with appropriate consideration for vulnerability flags.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS — FEES
=============================================================

Q: Is there a fee for making bank transfers online?
A: No. UK Faster Payments made via the mobile app or online banking are free on all accounts. CHAPS (same-day guaranteed transfer) costs £20 on the Everyday Account and is free on Premium.

Q: Am I charged for using my Meridian Bank card at another bank's ATM?
A: Meridian Bank does not charge a fee for using your card at any UK ATM. The ATM operator (another bank) may charge their own fee — if so, it will be displayed on screen before you confirm the withdrawal.

Q: What is the unarranged overdraft cap?
A: For Everyday Account customers, the unarranged overdraft fee of £5 per day is capped at £35 per month. You will never pay more than £35 in a single month from unarranged overdraft fees, regardless of how many days the account is in unarranged overdraft.

Q: Are there any fees for having a savings account?
A: No. Instant Access Savings Accounts and Cash ISAs have no monthly fees, no withdrawal fees, and no closure fees.

Q: How much does it cost to make an international payment?
A: £10 flat fee for SWIFT international transfers on the Everyday Account. Free on the Premium Account. The exchange rate includes a 2.75% margin (Everyday) or 0.75% margin (Premium) above the interbank rate.
