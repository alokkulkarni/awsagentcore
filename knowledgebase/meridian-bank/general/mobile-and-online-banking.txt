MERIDIAN BANK — MOBILE APP AND ONLINE BANKING
Knowledge Base Document | Version 1.0

=============================================================
OVERVIEW
=============================================================

Meridian Bank provides two digital banking channels:

1. Meridian Bank Mobile App — available free on iOS (iPhone/iPad, iOS 14+) and Android (6.0+). Download from the Apple App Store or Google Play Store by searching "Meridian Bank".
2. Meridian Bank Online Banking — browser-based at https://online.meridianbank.co.uk. Supported browsers: Chrome 90+, Safari 14+, Firefox 88+, Edge 90+.

Both channels offer the same core features. The mobile app has additional features such as instant card freeze, mobile cheque deposit, and biometric login.

=============================================================
GETTING STARTED — MOBILE APP
=============================================================

DOWNLOADING AND INSTALLING:
1. Open the App Store (iPhone) or Google Play Store (Android).
2. Search for "Meridian Bank" — look for the teal and white logo.
3. Tap "Get" (iOS) or "Install" (Android). The app is free.
4. Once installed, tap the app icon on your home screen.

REGISTERING FOR THE FIRST TIME:
1. On the app home screen, tap "Register".
2. Enter your 8-digit account number and 6-digit sort code (60-00-01).
3. Enter your date of birth and the postcode registered on your account.
4. Create a 6-digit app passcode (used to log in quickly each time).
5. You will receive a one-time passcode (OTP) by SMS to your registered mobile number. Enter it to verify.
6. Optionally, set up biometric login (Face ID, Touch ID, or fingerprint) when prompted.
7. You are now registered. Tap "Continue" to go to your account overview.

If you do not receive the SMS OTP within 3 minutes: check your signal and tap "Resend code". If the problem continues, call 0161 900 9000.

LOGGING IN (RETURNING USERS):
- Enter your 6-digit passcode, OR
- Use Face ID / Touch ID / fingerprint if enabled.
- After 3 incorrect passcode attempts, the app locks. Unlock by calling 0161 900 9000 or visiting a branch.

=============================================================
GETTING STARTED — ONLINE BANKING
=============================================================

REGISTERING FOR THE FIRST TIME:
1. Go to https://online.meridianbank.co.uk.
2. Click "Register" on the homepage.
3. Enter your account number, sort code, date of birth, and registered postcode.
4. Choose a username (6–20 characters, letters and numbers only).
5. Create a password (minimum 10 characters; must include uppercase, lowercase, a number, and a special character).
6. Set up three memorable questions for account recovery.
7. Verify via OTP sent to your registered mobile number or email.
8. Registration is complete. Click "Continue to login".

LOGGING IN (RETURNING USERS):
1. Go to https://online.meridianbank.co.uk.
2. Enter your username and password.
3. For high-value actions (payments over £500, adding new payees), you will be asked to complete a second factor: OTP via SMS or the mobile app's in-app approval notification.

MOBILE APP — NAVIGATION AND FEATURES
=============================================================

HOME SCREEN (DASHBOARD):
- Shows all accounts and cards linked to your profile: current account, savings account, credit card, and mortgage (if applicable).
- Each product tile shows the current balance or outstanding amount.
- Tap any product tile to open its detail screen.
- A quick-action bar at the bottom of the home screen shows: Home | Payments | Cards | More.

ACCOUNTS SECTION:
Navigation: Home screen → tap your Current Account or Savings Account tile.

Features available:
- Balance: current balance and available balance shown at the top.
- Transactions: scrollable list of transactions in date order (most recent first). Each transaction shows: date, merchant name, amount (debit/credit), and running balance.
  - Tap any transaction to see full detail: category, merchant logo, location (if available), and a "Dispute this transaction" option.
  - Search: tap the magnifying glass icon to search transactions by keyword, merchant name, or amount.
  - Filter: tap the filter icon to filter by date range, transaction type (debit/credit), or amount range.
- Sort code and account number: scroll to the bottom of the account detail screen. Tap "Show account details" to reveal full sort code and account number. This can be copied or shared.
- Statements: tap "Statements" → select the month and year → the PDF opens in-app. Tap the share icon to email or save the PDF.
- Download transactions: tap "Download" → choose CSV or PDF format and date range.

SAVINGS ACCOUNT:
Navigation: Home screen → tap your Savings Account tile.
- Shows current balance, interest rate (AER), and interest earned this year.
- Tap "Move money" to transfer funds between your current and savings account.
- Tap "Transactions" for a full history of deposits and withdrawals.
- Tap "Account details" for sort code, account number, and IBAN (for receiving international transfers into savings).

PAYMENTS:
Navigation: Bottom navigation bar → "Payments".

Sub-sections:
1. New payment: Pay someone new or an existing payee.
   - Enter payee sort code and account number, or select from saved payees.
   - Enter amount and optional reference.
   - Tap "Confirm" → authenticate with passcode/biometrics.
   - Payments under £500 to saved payees complete instantly (Faster Payments).
   - Payments over £500 or to new payees require OTP approval.
2. Standing orders: View, edit, or cancel existing standing orders. Set up a new one by tapping "+".
   - New standing order fields: Payee, sort code, account number, amount, start date, frequency (weekly/monthly/annually), end date (optional).
3. Direct debits: View all active direct debits. Tap any to see the mandate details and cancel if needed. Note: cancelling does not cancel the underlying contract with the company.
4. International payments: Send money abroad via SWIFT. Enter IBAN, BIC, recipient name, address, currency, and amount. Exchange rates are shown before confirmation. £10 flat fee for SWIFT transfers. Available for amounts up to £25,000 per transaction.
5. Bill payments: Pay utility bills, council tax, and HMRC directly using the billers' reference numbers. Separate from bank transfers — uses the Bacs billing system.

CARD CONTROLS (DEBIT AND CREDIT CARDS):
Navigation: Bottom navigation bar → "Cards".

Features:
- View all cards (debit and credit) linked to your profile.
- Tap a card to see:
  - Card status (Active / Frozen / Blocked)
  - Card number (last four digits shown; full number never displayed)
  - Expiry date (masked)
  - Available limit or balance

FREEZE / UNFREEZE CARD:
- Tap the card → tap "Freeze card" toggle (green = active, grey = frozen).
- A frozen card cannot be used for payments, ATM withdrawals, or online purchases.
- Freezing is instant and reversible. It does not affect direct debits linked to the card.
- Use freeze if you have temporarily misplaced your card but are not certain it is lost or stolen.

REPORT LOST OR STOLEN:
- Tap the card → tap "Report lost or stolen" → select "Lost" or "Stolen".
- Confirm the action. The card is permanently cancelled and a replacement ordered.
- Replacement card arrives in 3–5 working days (7–10 for international addresses).
- Emergency same-day card collection available at branches for Stolen cards — call 0161 900 9000.

PIN SERVICES:
- Tap "View PIN" to display your PIN securely in the app (authenticated with biometrics/passcode).
- Tap "Change PIN" to generate a new PIN. The new PIN activates when you next use your card at an ATM or chip-and-PIN terminal.

SPENDING CONTROLS:
- Tap "Spending controls" on a debit or credit card.
- Toggle on/off: contactless payments, online transactions, ATM withdrawals, international transactions.
- Set a daily spending limit (can be set lower than the bank's default; cannot be set higher without calling us).

APPLE PAY / GOOGLE PAY:
- Tap "Add to Apple Wallet" or "Add to Google Pay" directly from the card detail screen.
- Follow the wallet prompts. A verification OTP is sent by SMS.

CREDIT CARD FEATURES:
Navigation: Home screen → tap your Credit Card tile.
- Balance and available credit shown at the top.
- Minimum payment due and next payment date.
- Tap "Make a payment" to pay off some or all of the balance from your Meridian Bank current account.
  - Options: minimum payment, statement balance, full balance, or custom amount.
  - Payments made before 9pm on a business day are credited the same day.
- Tap "Transactions" for a full transaction history.
- Tap "Manage direct debit" to set up or change an automatic monthly payment.
- Tap "Request credit limit change" — applications are subject to credit assessment.

MORTGAGE FEATURES:
Navigation: Home screen → tap your Mortgage tile.
- Shows outstanding balance, next monthly payment date and amount, interest rate, and remaining term.
- Tap "Payment history" to see the last 24 months of mortgage payments.
- Tap "Make an overpayment" to pay extra off your mortgage. Your annual overpayment allowance is shown (typically 10% of outstanding balance per year without early repayment charge).
  - Enter overpayment amount → confirm. Overpayments are applied within 2 working days.
- Tap "Download mortgage statement" to get a PDF showing the full mortgage account history.
- Tap "Request redemption statement" — a formal statement of the amount required to repay your mortgage in full. Delivered by email within 2 working days.

NOTIFICATIONS:
Navigation: More → Notifications → Notification preferences.
- Toggle on/off: transaction alerts (every payment in/out), low balance alerts (set your own threshold), payment due reminders (credit card and mortgage), promotional messages.
- Notifications appear as push notifications on your phone and/or in the in-app notification centre.
- Tap any notification to jump directly to the related account or transaction.

INBOX / SECURE MESSAGES:
Navigation: More → Inbox.
- Send and receive secure messages to/from Meridian Bank (alternative to calling).
- Response time: within 2 working hours during business hours (Monday–Friday 8am–8pm, Saturday 8am–4pm).
- Attach documents or screenshots (max 10MB per message).
- All messages are stored securely and encrypted. Never send passwords or full card numbers via secure message.

DOCUMENTS AND LETTERS:
Navigation: More → Documents.
- Access all letters and notices sent by Meridian Bank: annual summaries, tax certificates (for savings and ISAs), overdraft notices, credit limit change letters, and general correspondence.
- Documents available going back 7 years.
- Download as PDF.

PROFILE AND SETTINGS:
Navigation: More → Profile.
- View and update: registered email address, registered mobile number, correspondence address.
  - Address change requires identity verification (upload a document or visit branch for high-value changes).
- Change app passcode: More → Security → Change passcode.
- Manage biometric login: More → Security → Biometric settings.
- Linked accounts: see all accounts tied to your profile and request to add or remove products.

HELP AND SUPPORT IN-APP:
Navigation: More → Help.
- Searchable FAQ covering the most common questions.
- Live chat: tap "Chat with us" (available Monday–Friday 8am–8pm, Saturday 8am–4pm).
- Call us: tap "Call us" to be connected directly to 0161 900 9000 without having to dial.
- Locate branch: tap "Find a branch" → enter postcode → shows nearest branches with opening hours and directions (opens in Maps).

ONLINE BANKING — NAVIGATION AND FEATURES
=============================================================

DASHBOARD (HOME PAGE AFTER LOGIN):
- Left-hand navigation menu: Accounts | Payments | Cards | Mortgages | Messages | Documents | Profile.
- Main area shows account summary tiles for all products.
- Alerts banner at top shows any important messages (e.g. pending payments, overdraft warnings).

ACCOUNTS:
Navigation: Left menu → "Accounts" → select Current Account or Savings Account.
- Balance summary panel at top (available balance, current balance, overdraft limit if applicable).
- Transactions table: date, description, debit, credit, balance. Default shows last 30 days.
  - Change date range: use the date pickers above the table.
  - Search: type in the search box to filter transactions by keyword or amount.
  - Export: click "Download" → CSV, OFX (for accounting software), or PDF. OFX format is compatible with QuickBooks, Xero, Sage.
- Account details: click "Account details" tab to see sort code, account number, IBAN, and BIC.
- Statements: click "Statements" tab → select month from dropdown → view or download PDF.

PAYMENTS:
Navigation: Left menu → "Payments".

Sub-sections (tabs at the top of the Payments page):
1. New payment:
   - Pay a saved payee or add a new payee.
   - New payee: enter name, sort code, and account number. An OTP is required to add a new payee (security step).
   - After payee is added, enter amount, reference, and payment date (today or a future date).
   - For payments over £500: OTP from SMS or mobile app in-app approval required.
2. Manage payees: view, edit, or delete saved payees.
3. Standing orders: view active standing orders. Click "New standing order" to set one up.
4. Direct debits: view active direct debits. Click "Cancel" next to any to remove.
5. Scheduled payments: view all future-dated payments and standing orders due in the next 30 days.
6. International transfers: full SWIFT transfer form. Currency calculator built in; shows exchange rate and total GBP cost before you confirm.

CARDS:
Navigation: Left menu → "Cards".
- View all cards: debit and credit, with current status.
- Online banking card controls are a subset of the mobile app. Full freeze/unfreeze is only available in the mobile app for security reasons.
- On the web you can: report a card as lost or stolen, request a replacement, view PIN reminder (sent by post), and set spending controls.

MORTGAGE:
Navigation: Left menu → "Mortgages".
- All information from the app is also available here.
- Additional feature in online banking: mortgage calculator — enter a hypothetical balance, rate, and term to see what your payments would be. Useful when considering overpayments.

MESSAGES:
Navigation: Left menu → "Messages".
- Same secure messaging as the app inbox. Replies are sent here and to the email on your profile.

DOCUMENTS:
Navigation: Left menu → "Documents".
- All letters and notices available here, organised by date. Filter by document type.

PROFILE:
Navigation: Left menu → "Profile".
- Update personal details: contact number, email, address.
- Security settings: change password, update memorable questions, manage trusted devices.
- Trusted devices: devices that are pre-approved for login without OTP. Remove a device here if a device is lost or you no longer recognise it.
- Paperless preferences: opt in/out of paperless statements and letters.

SECURITY FEATURES
=============================================================

BIOMETRIC LOGIN (APP ONLY):
Set up via More → Security → Biometric settings. Uses the device's built-in Face ID, Touch ID, or fingerprint scanner. Meridian Bank never stores your biometric data — this is handled entirely by your device.

TWO-FACTOR AUTHENTICATION (2FA):
Active on all accounts for high-value payments, new payee additions, and profile changes. OTP delivered via SMS or via in-app notification (push notification that you approve on the mobile app, faster than SMS).

FRAUD ALERTS:
Meridian Bank's systems monitor all accounts 24/7. If unusual activity is detected you will receive an immediate push notification or SMS. Respond "Yes it was me" or "No it was not me" directly in the message. If "No": your card is temporarily frozen and the fraud team is alerted.

STRONG CUSTOMER AUTHENTICATION (SCA):
Required by UK financial regulations for online purchases over £30 and for all new payee setups. You will be redirected to a 3D Secure screen for card-not-present purchases, where you approve the transaction via push notification or OTP.

TROUBLESHOOTING
=============================================================

APP NOT LOADING:
1. Check your internet connection.
2. Close and reopen the app.
3. Check whether a Meridian Bank service update is in progress at status.meridianbank.co.uk.
4. Delete and reinstall the app (your account data is not stored locally; you will need to re-register but your transactions are not affected).

FORGOTTEN PASSCODE:
1. On the app login screen, tap "Forgot passcode".
2. Re-verify your identity (account number, sort code, date of birth, postcode).
3. Receive OTP by SMS.
4. Create a new 6-digit passcode.

FORGOTTEN ONLINE BANKING PASSWORD:
1. On the login page, click "Forgot password".
2. Enter your username.
3. Answer your memorable questions.
4. Receive a reset link by email (valid 30 minutes).
5. Create a new password.
If you cannot remember your memorable questions: call 0161 900 9000.

NOT RECEIVING OTP:
1. Check your mobile signal.
2. Verify that your registered mobile number is correct (log in to online banking → Profile, or call us).
3. Request the OTP to be resent (wait 60 seconds before retrying).
4. As an alternative, use the in-app approval notification if you have the mobile app installed.

SUSPECTED FRAUD OR UNAUTHORISED ACCESS:
Call the 24/7 fraud line: 0161 900 9005. You can also freeze all cards immediately in the mobile app (Cards → select card → Freeze).
