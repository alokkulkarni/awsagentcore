MERIDIAN BANK — DEBIT CARDS
Knowledge Base Document | Version 1.0

=============================================================
OVERVIEW
=============================================================

Every Meridian Bank current account (Everyday and Premium) includes a Visa debit card. The card can be used anywhere that accepts Visa — in shops, online, abroad, and at ATMs worldwide.

=============================================================
CARD FEATURES
=============================================================

Card network:          Visa
Contactless limit:     £100 per single transaction
Daily ATM limit:       £500 (can be temporarily increased — see below)
Daily debit purchase:  £5,000 (default); can be reduced via spending controls in the app
Online payments:       Enabled by default; can be disabled via spending controls
International use:     Enabled by default; can be disabled via spending controls
Apple Pay / Google Pay: Supported — add via the mobile app (Cards → Add to wallet)
Chip and PIN:          Available on all cards; PIN can be changed via the app or at ATMs

Premium account cardholders also receive:
- No foreign exchange fee on card purchases abroad (Everyday: 2.75% FX fee applies)
- No fee on overseas ATM withdrawals up to £500/month (Everyday: £1.50 + 2.75% FX fee)

=============================================================
CONTACTLESS PAYMENTS
=============================================================

Tap your card (or phone/watch via Apple Pay/Google Pay) on any contactless terminal.

Limits:
- Per transaction: £100
- Cumulative contactless without PIN: after 5 consecutive contactless payments (or if total contactless spending exceeds £300 since last PIN entry), the terminal will prompt for chip-and-PIN to verify identity. This is a UK financial regulation requirement.

Apple Pay and Google Pay:
- No per-transaction limit when using biometric or device passcode authentication.
- Treat as chip-and-PIN for high-value contactless transactions.

To disable contactless:
- Mobile app: Cards → select debit card → Spending controls → toggle off Contactless.
- Contactless can be re-enabled instantly in the same place.

=============================================================
ATM WITHDRAWALS
=============================================================

UK ATM withdrawals:
- Free at all UK ATMs (including non-Meridian Bank ATMs — we do not charge a fee, but the ATM operator may charge a fee; this will be displayed on screen before you confirm).
- Daily ATM limit: £500.

Requesting a temporary ATM limit increase:
- Call 0161 900 9000 (Monday–Friday 8am–8pm, Saturday 8am–4pm).
- Verify your identity.
- The increase is valid for one transaction on the specified day. The limit returns to £500 automatically.
- Maximum one-off increase: up to £2,000 (subject to account balance and account standing).

Overseas ATM withdrawals:
- Everyday Account: £1.50 transaction fee plus 2.75% foreign exchange fee on the GBP equivalent.
- Premium Account: free up to £500/month, then £1 per withdrawal, no FX fee.
- Check terminal for any local ATM operator fees (charged by the local bank — outside our control).

=============================================================
SPENDING CONTROLS (MOBILE APP)
=============================================================

Navigation: Mobile app → Cards → select debit card → Spending controls.

Toggle on/off:
- Contactless payments
- Online and telephone transactions (card-not-present)
- ATM withdrawals
- International transactions (useful when travelling — enable before you go, disable on return)

Set daily spending limit:
- Tap "Set daily limit" and enter an amount lower than the bank's default (£5,000).
- Useful for budgeting or reducing risk if you are concerned about fraud.
- To increase back to the default, call 0161 900 9000.

=============================================================
FREEZING YOUR CARD
=============================================================

Use freeze if you think you have temporarily misplaced your card but are not certain it is lost or stolen.

How to freeze:
- Mobile app: Cards → select debit card → tap "Freeze card" toggle (turns grey).
- A frozen card is declined for all transactions immediately.
- Tap again to unfreeze instantly.

What freeze does NOT affect:
- Pre-authorised direct debits linked to the card (these continue).
- Existing pending transactions (already authorised before the freeze — these complete normally).
- Your card number and account number remain the same.

Freeze is reversible. If you find your card and are confident it has not been used by anyone else, simply unfreeze in the app.

=============================================================
REPORTING A LOST OR STOLEN CARD
=============================================================

If your card is lost or stolen:
1. Mobile app: Cards → select debit card → "Report lost or stolen" → select "Lost" or "Stolen" → confirm.
2. Or call the 24/7 fraud and lost/stolen line: 0161 900 9005.

What happens next:
- Your card is permanently cancelled immediately.
- A replacement card is ordered automatically and delivered within 3–5 working days (to your registered address).
- If you are abroad: contact us on +44 161 900 9005. Emergency cash may be arranged in exceptional circumstances.
- Stolen card: if you believe your card has been used fraudulently, state this clearly. The fraud team will review your recent transactions and initiate a chargeback for any unauthorised payments.

Emergency in-branch replacement (stolen card only):
- Visit any Meridian Bank branch with photo ID.
- A same-day temporary card can be issued.
- Branches are open Monday–Friday 9am–5pm, Saturday 9am–1pm.

=============================================================
REPLACING A DAMAGED OR EXPIRED CARD
=============================================================

Expired cards:
- Replacement cards are sent automatically 4 weeks before the expiry date to your registered address.
- The new card arrives with the same account number (last four digits) and sort code, but a new expiry date and CVV.
- Activate by making any chip-and-PIN purchase or ATM withdrawal (contactless requires one chip-and-PIN use first).

Damaged cards:
- Mobile app: Cards → select debit card → "Request replacement" → "Damaged card".
- Or call 0161 900 9000.
- Replacement arrives in 3–5 working days.
- No fee for card replacement (one free replacement per 12-month period; subsequent replacements: £5 each).

=============================================================
CHANGING YOUR PIN
=============================================================

Option 1 (mobile app): Cards → select debit card → "PIN services" → "Change PIN" → authenticate with biometrics/passcode → a new PIN is generated securely. Activate the new PIN at any Meridian Bank ATM.

Option 2 (ATM): At any Meridian Bank ATM, select "PIN services" → "Change PIN" → enter current PIN → enter new PIN twice.

Option 3 (in branch): Ask a member of staff to generate a PIN reminder (sent to your registered address within 3 working days) or to assist with a PIN change at the branch terminal.

Forgotten PIN:
- Mobile app: Cards → select debit card → "View PIN" (authenticated by biometrics/passcode).
- This shows your current PIN securely on screen. It is not stored in the app — it is fetched securely each time.

=============================================================
CARD FRAUD AND DISPUTED TRANSACTIONS
=============================================================

If you see a transaction you do not recognise:
1. Check if it could be a merchant trading under a different name (e.g. a supermarket fuel station may show as the company's corporate name).
2. Mobile app: Transactions → tap the transaction → tap "Dispute this transaction".
3. Or call 0161 900 9005 (fraud line, 24/7).

Types of disputes:
- Unauthorised transaction (you did not make it): immediate card freeze, fraud investigation. Refund within 5 working days if confirmed as fraud. Provisional credit may be applied while the investigation is ongoing.
- Transaction amount incorrect: the merchant charged you more than agreed. Evidence (receipt, screenshot) speeds up resolution.
- Goods/services not received: e.g. you paid for an item that was not delivered. Section 75 protection applies for purchases between £100 and £30,000 made with a credit card. For debit card, chargeback through Visa applies (must be raised within 120 days of the transaction).
- Duplicate charge: same amount charged twice by the same merchant.

Do NOT dispute a transaction if: you made the purchase but regret it, or you cancelled a subscription (contact the company directly first).

=============================================================
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS — DEBIT CARDS
=============================================================

Q: My card was declined even though I have sufficient funds. Why?
A: Possible reasons: the merchant requires chip-and-PIN after a series of contactless taps; spending controls are restricting the transaction type; the merchant has a minimum transaction amount; your card is in an unusual location and a temporary security hold is in place. Call 0161 900 9000 for immediate assistance.

Q: Can I use my debit card abroad?
A: Yes, anywhere that accepts Visa. Fees apply for Everyday Account holders (2.75% FX + £1.50 ATM fee). Premium Account holders pay no fees up to £500/month ATM withdrawals. Notify us before travelling if you will be in a country outside your normal usage pattern — you can do this in the app (More → Travel notifications → Add trip).

Q: What should I do before travelling abroad?
A: Add a travel notification in the mobile app (More → Travel notifications) with your destination and travel dates. This reduces the chance of transactions being declined as suspicious. Enable international transactions in Spending Controls if you had them turned off.

Q: Is there a charge for using my card in foreign currency?
A: Everyday Account: 2.75% non-sterling transaction fee on purchases. Premium Account: no foreign exchange fee. Both accounts: using a dynamic currency conversion (DCC) option offered by a foreign merchant means they handle the conversion — always choose to pay in the local currency to get the better rate.

Q: How quickly does a declined authorisation fee return to my account?
A: Pre-authorisation holds (e.g. at hotels or petrol stations) are released within 7 days. Some merchants release within 1–2 days. Meridian Bank cannot speed up a hold placed by a merchant — contact the merchant directly to release it early.
