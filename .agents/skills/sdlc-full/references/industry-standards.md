# SDLC Full Pipeline Industry Standards

The `sdlc-full` skill is designed to align a production-quality end-to-end software delivery workflow with the following standards and reference models.

## DORA

- Source: [dora.dev](https://dora.dev)
- Core metrics: deployment frequency, lead time for changes, mean time to restore, change failure rate.
- Relevance: the full pipeline report should make delivery flow measurable and repeatable.

## Accelerate

- Source: *Accelerate* by Nicole Forsgren, Jez Humble, and Gene Kim.
- Relevance: emphasises high-performing engineering organisations, fast feedback loops, and measurable quality gates.

## SAFe

- Source: [scaledagileframework.com](https://scaledagileframework.com)
- Relevance: the refinement and architecture phases align with PI Planning, dependency management, and incremental delivery preparation.

## DevOps Handbook

- Source: *The DevOps Handbook* by Gene Kim, Jez Humble, Patrick Debois, and John Willis.
- Relevance: reinforces automation, continuous feedback, quality built into the pipeline, and shared ownership across delivery phases.

## Google Site Reliability Engineering

- Source: *Site Reliability Engineering* and *The Site Reliability Workbook*.
- Relevance: informs the review and test phases through reliability gates, error-budget thinking, and production-readiness discipline.

## agentskills.io

- Source: [agentskills.io](https://agentskills.io)
- Relevance: defines the packaging model for skills, references, templates, assets, and executable automation used by this pipeline skill.
